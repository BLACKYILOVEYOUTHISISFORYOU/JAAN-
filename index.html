Our Journey

Every step led me to you

How We First Met

We met as complete strangers on Instagram without knowing that one day we would become such an important part of each other’s lives. It started with simple conversations, random replies, and small talks, but slowly those conversations became longer and more meaningful. What was once just two unknown people talking online slowly turned into a beautiful connection that neither of us expected.

From Friends to Something More

As days passed, we started sharing everything with each other like best friends. We talked for hours about random things, our feelings, our daily lives, our happiness, and even our problems. You became a part of my routine without me even realizing it. Every notification from you made my day better, and every conversation with you brought me peace.

But then, when I saw you talking with other people, I started feeling jealous. That jealousy made me realize something important — you were no longer just a friend to me. I had already become emotionally attached to you, and the thought of losing your attention or importance in your life hurt me more than I expected. That’s when I understood my feelings were real.

The Day I Proposed You

Because of those feelings, I finally gathered the courage to tell you what was in my heart. I proposed to you, not just because I liked you, but because I genuinely wanted you in my life in a special way. And when you accepted my proposal, it became one of the happiest moments of my life.

From that day, our relationship started growing stronger. We created countless memories together, shared laughter, emotional moments, late-night talks, care, love, and support. Even though we were in a long-distance relationship, our bond became deeper day by day.

Our Journey Together

Our relationship was never perfect, but it was always real. We experienced happiness, sadness, misunderstandings, fights, tears, anger, and emotional moments — just like every true relationship does. Sometimes we hurt each other unintentionally, sometimes we overthought, and sometimes distance made things harder for us.

But despite everything, one thing always remained the same — we never gave up on each other. No matter how many fights we had or how difficult situations became, deep inside we both knew that our love was stronger than our problems. We always found our way back to each other because what we have is not temporary.

Our story started with two strangers on Instagram, but today it feels like destiny brought us together for a reason. And no matter how hard life gets, I know one thing for sure — I never want to lose you, because you have become one of the most precious parts of my life. ❤️
