01 / the letter

Dear
Jaan 😇,

FROM MY HEART

I still think it's funny that we started as two strangers on Instagram.

Somewhere along the way, your name became one of my favorite things to see on my screen. You became part of my routine, part of my thoughts, part of the random little things I wanted to tell someone.

“And suddenly, someone who wasn't even in my life became a piece of the life I imagined.”

We've had beautiful days and difficult ones. We've laughed, missed each other, misunderstood each other, gotten angry, made up, and found our way back. I know I have not always handled everything perfectly. I know some of my reactions have hurt you.

For this anniversary, I don't want to make a huge promise about becoming perfect overnight. I want to promise something more honest: I want to keep becoming better at loving you. More patience. Better communication. Less reacting. More listening. More reassurance. More peace.

Because you deserve to feel loved, not just hear that you are loved.

And if someday we're looking back at this page from a completely different life, I hope we laugh at how dramatic we were and remember that this was the little corner of the internet where I tried to tell you how much you meant to me.

Happy second anniversary, my girl. Thank you for every tiny moment that became a memory.

Love,
Kalyan
